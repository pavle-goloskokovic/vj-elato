VJ Elato

Two-tab browser VJ. One tab plays, the other controls. No server, no install.

1 · Open player 2 · Open controller
  1. Open the player, drag it to the projector, click it for fullscreen.
  2. Open the controller in another tab of the same browser.
  3. Pick your clip folder for local files, or play straight from Destockd and archive.org.

Footage: public domain films indexed by Destockd and Spelunkr, hosted by the Internet Archive. Please support them.
